Lagoon Amusement Park

What is Lagoon?

Lagoon is a place in Farmington, Utah that is an enjoyable amusement park to many people. Lagoon is located In northern Utah where the weather is hotter and the landscape is more green. Lagoon has many trees and plants and they are very helpful for shade. It also has animals walking through the park like peacocks. Lagoon has many fun rides that many people love riding. These reasons all give Lagoon a place you should add to your visit list.

Reasons that you should visit

  1. Lagoon is family friendly and has many rides for younger kids to go on
  2. Lagoon has many fast and fun rides like cannibal that goes up to 70 miles per hour!
  3. Lagoon is a good place to visit with your friends because many of the rides are multiseater rides
  4. Lagoon has lots trees providing you with lots of shade on hot and sunny dayd
  5. Lagoon has many great food and desert spots that are very good and yummy

Lagoons waterpark!

Lagoon-A-Beach is a six-acre water park located right inside Lagoon Amusement Park. It is a great place to cool off on a hot summer day, featuring 10 different water slides that range from fast, 65-foot drops like Liquid Lightning to twisting tube rides like the Pipeline. If you want to relax instead, you can grab a tube and float past waterfalls and shady tunnels on the Moochs Mainstream lazy river, or go for a swim in the massive Blue Lagoon pool. Younger kids even have their own shallow play zone with mini-slides at Bongos Bay, making it easy for everyone to take a refreshing break from the roller coasters without buying a separate ticket.
